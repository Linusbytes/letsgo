School Vision, Mission and History

Our Mission

We partner with communities in Cambodia to break the cycle of poverty. Through quality education, skills training, and hands-on support, we help people build better lives for themselves and their families.

Every person deserves the chance to succeed, no matter how difficult their situation. We believe that when individuals grow stronger, entire communities transform.

Our Vision

We dream of a world where every person can live with dignity, respect, and reach their full potential.

We’re building an inclusive community at The Learners’ Village in rural Cambodia. Here, people learn job skills and life skills that help them create better futures for themselves and their families.
